**Restaurant Industry Analysis: Insights from Zomato Dataset**

**Objective:**
Identify key factors affecting restaurant success and recommend strategies for menu design, geographic expansion, and customer engagement using Zomato data.
Derive actionable insights to guide strategic business decisions.


**Dataset Overview**:
Sources: Zomato.csv and Country-Code.xlsx.


**Key Features:** Restaurant ID, Restaurant Name, Country Code, City, Address, Locality, Locality Verbose, Longitude, Latitude, Cuisines, Average Cost for two, Currency, Has Table booking, Has Online delivery, Is delivering now, Switch to order menu, Price range, Aggregate rating, Rating color, Rating text, Votes, Country.
Data Cleaning and Preparation:
Handled missing values and inconsistencies.
Standardized cost formats.
Split and analyzed multi-cuisine fields.
Merged country codes with country names.
Identified and treated outliers in votes and cost.

**Business Interpretations:**

**Cuisine Strategy:**
Focus on popular cuisines like North Indian, Chinese, and Fast Food to maximize reach.
Experiment with emerging cuisines like Cafes and Desserts to capture urban customer interest.


**Country Expansion Strategy:**
Target highly engaged markets like Indonesia, UAE, and Turkey.
Consider emerging opportunities in the Philippines and Sri Lanka.


**Pricing Strategy:**
Emphasize quality and service over just high pricing to improve customer ratings.
Offer value-driven menu options to appeal to a broader customer base.


**Strategic Recommendations:**
Launch multi-cuisine restaurants that blend traditional and international flavors.
Focus marketing and loyalty programs in countries with high engagement.
Promote customer reviews and ratings to boost online visibility.
Consider low-cost fast food models alongside premium dining options.

**Limitations:**
Dataset is historic and may not reflect 2025 trends.
Votes are self-reported and can be biased.
Cost estimates may vary by region and currency fluctuations.

**Conclusion:**
Traditional cuisines dominate, but customer tastes are diversifying.
Engagement levels vary by country, necessitating localized strategies.
Data-driven insights are essential for successful restaurant business planning.

**Future Scope:** 
Integrate customer reviews text analysis to better understand customer sentiment beyond ratings alone.
